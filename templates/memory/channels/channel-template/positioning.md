# Channel Positioning & Editorial Policy

## Target Audience
- **Primary Persona**: [Who is reading/watching? What is their current knowledge level?]
- **Pains & Desires**: [What problems do they want solved? What aspirational goals do they have?]

## Tone of Voice
- [e.g. Authoritative, direct, research-led, practical, zero corporate fluff]
- **Forbidden Patterns**: No sensationalist clickbait ("You won't believe this!"), no fake intimacy, no generic AI phrasing.

## Content Pillars & Formats
1. **Educational / Breakdown**: Deep dive with calculations or code snippets.
2. **Case Study / Real Experience**: Real experiments, failures, and learnings.
3. **News & Commentary**: Quick takes on fresh industry announcements.
